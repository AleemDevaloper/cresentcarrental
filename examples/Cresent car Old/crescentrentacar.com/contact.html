Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our car rental services

What documents do I need to rent a car?

To rent a car, you'll need a valid driver's license, CNIC/passport, and a credit/debit card for the security deposit.

Is there a security deposit required?

Yes, we require a security deposit which is refundable upon return of the vehicle in the same condition as it was rented.

Do you offer airport pickup and drop-off services?

Yes, we offer airport pickup and drop-off services at Lahore International Airport for an additional fee.

What happens if I return the car late?

Late returns are charged at an hourly rate. If you know you'll be late, please contact us as soon as possible.

Can I extend my rental period?

Yes, you can extend your rental period by contacting our customer service team. Extension is subject to availability.

Do you provide the driver?

Yes, we provide the driver upon request for an additional fee. Please request this at the time of booking.
